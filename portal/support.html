Frequently Asked Questions

How do I renew my membership?
You can renew your membership through the 'Membership Details' page. Click on 'Renew Early' or wait for the renewal button to appear 30 days before expiry.
Where can I find the Solar PV technical standards?
All technical standards are located in the 'Resources' library under the 'Technical' category.
Can I post products on the Marketplace?
Yes! All active KEREA members have basic marketplace access. Navigate to the Workplace and click 'Add Listing'.
